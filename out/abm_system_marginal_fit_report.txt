likelihood.

400 quarters each, 8 laws, 34.5 minutes on 16 threads.

that law had the lowest Schwarz criterion, over the series where it
converged. 'gain' is its mean log-likelihood less the Gaussian's on the
same series, so the Gaussian's own gain is zero by construction.
