Whether the cross-section of firms behind GDP is one a central limit theorem
acts on.

5 configurations of the design, seeds 1 to 5, periods 201 to the end of
each run. The two firm types are kept apart.
Anderson-Darling null simulated from 100000 Gaussian samples at each firm
count, seed 20260923.

PART 1. The cross-section of output levels at a date, averaged over the
periods of a run and then
over the runs. Concentration is H * N: 1 when every firm is the same size,
N when one firm is everything. AD<.05 is 0.05 under a Gaussian
cross-section.

  firm type      N   runs   skewness  exc kurt  concentr   AD<.05
  K-firms       20     25     2.6201    7.1019     5.757   0.9384
  C-firms      200     25    -0.9945    1.9339     1.066   0.9982

Whether the terms move together, and what the sum inherits. The average
correlation is over the pairs of firm output changes within a run. The
effective count is N / (1 + (N - 1) rho). A sum of m independent draws
from the cross-section would have skewness gamma / sqrt(m) and excess
kurtosis kappa / m.

  firm type      N   mean rho  effective N   sum skew at N  sum kurt at N  sum kurt at eff
  K-firms       20    -0.0024        20.94          0.5859         0.3551           0.3392
  C-firms      200    -0.0008       236.37         -0.0703         0.0097           0.0082

PART 2. The cross-section of changes, which is what GDP's change is a sum
of. Averaged over the periods of a run and then over the runs.

  firm type      N   runs   exc kurt of change  concentr of |change|
  K-firms       20     25               3.6034                 3.028
  C-firms      200     25              14.4586                 3.103

GDP growth three ways, over the 400 quarters of each run, averaged over the
runs. 'as it stands' is the series dataset/abm_system reports.
'standardised' divides each quarter by sqrt(N) times that quarter's own
cross-sectional spread, which is what a central limit theorem acting at
each date separately would leave. 'dispersion alone' is the excess
kurtosis a Gaussian whose variance moved like this one's would have.

  quantity                    skewness   exc kurt
  GDP growth as it stands       0.0093     0.5711
  standardised                  0.0777     0.1871
  dispersion alone                         0.6345
