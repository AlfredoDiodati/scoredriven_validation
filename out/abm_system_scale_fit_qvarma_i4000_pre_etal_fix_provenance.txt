Consequence, stated so a later reader does not have to reconstruct it. The
per-period density term in that copy was evaluated as log(nu + q_t) with the
log(nu) half folded into the constant, and the normalization as two lgamma
terms subtracted. Both are exact identities and both lose the answer at a
large nu. Fits in this tree that reached a large nu are therefore on an
artifact rather than on the likelihood - 11 of them carry nu above 1e10.

More importantly for any comparison: the objective agrees with the corrected
one to about 1e-8 wherever nu is moderate, but a perturbation that small is
amplified by an unconverged search. Measured on this project's own data, two
builds agreeing to 6e-13 in the gradient produced a mean |difference| of 7.2
in the fitted log-likelihood across 10,800 series, against a mean gain of 1.45
for the entire 2000-to-4000 budget increase. A comparison between this tree
and one fitted after the correction is therefore dominated by the build, not
by the budget.
