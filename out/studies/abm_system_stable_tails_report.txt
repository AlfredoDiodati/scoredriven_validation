Whether the macro series are stable with index below 2, or have a finite
variance.

Columns. 'var slope' is the slope of log sample variance on log n over
n = 50 to 400: 0 for a finite variance, 2/alpha - 1 for a stable alpha.
'Hill' is the tail index from the largest 10, 5 and 2.5 per cent of |x|.
'iqr slope' and 'sd slope' are the slope of log scale on log block length
over k = 1 to 32: 1/2 for a finite variance with independent terms,
1/alpha for a stable alpha.

PART 1. Calibration. 20000 samples of 400 values from each law, seed 20260923.
A stable alpha should give var slope 2/alpha - 1, Hill alpha, and
iqr slope 1/alpha. The Gaussian row is alpha = 2: 0, no power-law tail,
and 0.5.
