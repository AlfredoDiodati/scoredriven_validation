t-QVARMA parameter recovery study
float64 build, 12 replications per cell

Each cell simulates from known parameters, fits from a start perturbed
by 0.25 in every coordinate, and reports the root mean squared error of
the estimates on the unconstrained scale, per parameter block, averaged
over the replications that converged. conv is how many of them did.
se is how many of those produced usable standard errors, and cover the
fraction of coordinates whose true value fell within 1.96 of them, which
should be near 0.95 wherever the errors are honest.
Smaller is better; the last column names the block that came out worst.
