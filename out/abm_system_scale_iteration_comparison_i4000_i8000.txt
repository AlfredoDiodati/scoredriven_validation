Fits left out

  nu enters as exp(theta) + 2, and past nu of about 3.6e9 the coded
  log-likelihood loses precision - the density's (nu + K)/2 log(1 + q/nu)
  term is a cancellation of two large quantities - while the likelihood
  itself is flat there, at the Gaussian limit the t tends to. L-BFGS climbs
  what is left, and a larger budget gives it more room to. Fits carrying
  nu above 1e+10 at either budget are left out of everything below.

  left out                    12 of 500000 (0.0024%)
  already over the limit at 4000  2
  over the limit at 8000         12
  compared below              499988
